## Core strategy

LeCrown should use five coordinated routes to revenue:

1. **Direct government work:** register with the buyer, monitor its procurement
   channel, and submit only when the scope, experience, capacity, and economics
   pass the solicitation review matrix.
2. **Subcontracting:** identify active prime contractors with small-business
   subcontracting goals, find both their supplier-diversity contact and the
   program manager closest to the work, and approach them around a specific
   contract or capability gap.
3. **Certification-driven access:** pursue certifications only when they open a
   defined buyer, directory, set-aside, mentor-protege, or supplier-diversity
   channel.
4. **Institutional and local buyers:** develop repeatable entry paths for
   universities, transit, cities, counties, school systems, ports, and regional
   authorities instead of treating every portal as a one-off.
5. **Commercial supplier diversity:** use women-owned and other applicable
   credentials to reach corporate procurement programs in addition to public
   contracting.

Certifications create access; they do not create demand by themselves. Every
certification lane should therefore be linked to:

- target buyers and prime contractors;
- relevant NAICS and NIGP codes;
- a buyer-specific capability statement;
- proof documents and renewal dates;
- current opportunities and outreach activity;
- an accountable owner and next action.

## Market-access map

### Federal

| Route | What it unlocks | Immediate LeCrown use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAM.gov registration, UEI, and Small Business Search profile | Basic federal eligibility and discoverability by agencies and primes | Confirm the registration is active, align NAICS codes and keywords, and make the capabilities narrative consistent with the capability statement |
| WOSB/EDWOSB | Eligible federal set-asides and a recognized women-owned route | Run an ownership, control, citizenship, NAICS, and economic-disadvantage eligibility screen before applying |
| 8(a) Business Development | Business development support and eligible set-aside/sole-source paths | Ask UH APEX to review eligibility and timing before committing to the application |
| HUBZone | Eligible HUBZone set-asides and prime-contractor goal credit | Check the principal office and employee-residency requirements against the current SBA map and rules |
| VOSB/SDVOSB | Veteran contracting channels | Treat as conditional; pursue only if ownership, control, and veteran-status requirements are met |
| Mentor-Protege and joint ventures | Capacity building and team-based pursuit of larger work | Identify mentors already serving LeCrown's target agencies and capabilities |
| SUBNet and prime-contractor directories | Direct subcontract listings and primes with subcontracting plans | Feed opportunities into the platform funnel and build a named-prime account list |

## Opportunity-to-revenue operating loop

1. **Discover:** pull public opportunities, RFQs, SUBNet listings, forecasts,
   buyer events, and prime-contractor targets into one funnel.
2. **Qualify:** use the solicitation matrix to assess mandatory requirements,
   scope fit, past performance, capital, competition, delivery risk, pricing,
   and probability of win.
3. **Select the route:** prime, subcontractor, teaming partner, mentor-protege,
   supplier-diversity introduction, or no-bid.
4. **Prepare:** assemble the reusable evidence packet and tailor the capability
   statement, references, past performance, and outreach to the buyer or prime.
5. **Engage:** contact the buyer, small-business liaison, prime supplier-
   diversity office, and relevant program manager; attend mandatory events.
6. **Track:** record contacts, next actions, deadlines, documents, and status in
   the platform/CRM.
7. **Learn:** record why LeCrown won, lost, or declined and use the result to
   refine keywords, buyers, certifications, and capability statements.

## Evidence and safety rules

- Verify all certification requirements, processing times, and program status
  against the official site before making an application decision.
- Keep credentials, tax returns, personal net-worth statements, citizenship
  records, and ownership evidence outside Git.
- Store only document names, status, owners, renewal dates, and secure-location
  references in the platform.
- Preserve a source URL and checked date for every buyer, certification, and
  program claim.
- Treat a portal account, submitted application, certification approval,
  qualified opportunity, proposal, and awarded work as distinct states.

Official program pages were checked on July 22, 2026. The source documents are
valuable strategy references, but they are not proof that a program requirement
or eligibility rule is current.
