## Reading notes: Simplified Government Subcontracting Guide

The guide recommends subcontracting as a lower-barrier way to build government
experience and past performance while learning under an established prime. Its
operating sequence is:

1. Establish the entity and registration foundation: SAM, SBA profile, UEI,
   NAICS, PSC, state/local bidder registration, NIGP codes, and approved
   certifications.
2. Prepare a concise, targeted capability statement.
3. Perform market research using federal award data, state/local bid systems,
   prime-contractor directories, subcontracting-plan directories, and industry
   events.
4. Contact the prime's Small Business Liaison Office or supplier portal with a
   specific capability-to-contract fit.
5. Use a written teaming or subcontract agreement and manage flow-down clauses,
   performance, and records.

Practical interpretation for LeCrown: do not send a generic "we do IT" note.
Target a known prime, agency, contract family, and technical problem; then lead
with secure data/AI/cloud delivery outcomes that complement the prime's scope.
