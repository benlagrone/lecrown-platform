  is verified for the same legal entity.

  performance.

  reviewed target, verified credentials, and explicit approval.

  outreach message.

## Weekly operating cadence

### Monday - pipeline and opportunity scan

- Review SAM, SBA/DSBS, USAspending, Texas ESBD, METRO, City of Houston, and
  priority buyer portals.
- Log only relevant opportunities with buyer, due date, codes, fit, and next
  decision.

### Tuesday - registrations and evidence

- Complete one approved portal profile or evidence-packaging task.
- Save confirmation and update the tracker immediately.

### Wednesday - prime research and outreach

- Advance two prime targets.
- Customize the capability statement and outreach to a named opportunity,
  agency, or contract vehicle.

### Thursday - certifications and document packet

- Work the City OBO packet and the selected federal certification lane.
- Keep sensitive values in the approved private store, not public CSV files.

### Friday - follow-up and scorecard

- Follow up on messages sent five business days earlier.
- Reconcile tracker status to proof.
- Set the following week's three most important actions.
