# LeCrown Subcontracting and Market-Access Plan

## Outcome

Build a repeatable government and supplier-diversity growth system that turns
registrations, certifications, buyer relationships, prime-contractor
relationships, and opportunity monitoring into qualified pursuits and awarded
work.

This plan uses the [Government Contracting Knowledge Base](./README.md) and the
[Solicitation Review Matrix](./source-materials/solicitation-services-products-review-matrix.xlsx)
as its operating references.

## Success measures

Track these separately so activity is not mistaken for revenue:

| Measure | 30-day target | 90-day target |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Foundational registrations verified and documented | 3 | All applicable |
| Certification lanes with an eligibility decision and next action | 4 | All priority lanes |
| Named target buyers | 10 | 20 |
| Named target prime contractors | 10 | 25 |
| Buyer/prime contacts with a dated next action | 10 | 30 |
| Qualified opportunities reviewed with the matrix | 4 | 15 |
| Targeted capability statements/outreach packets | 3 | 8 |
| Prime or subcontract submissions | 1 | 5 |
| Awards or formal teaming agreements | Baseline | 1+ |

Targets are management goals, not forecasts or promises.

## Workstream 1: Establish the company truth set

**Timing:** Week 1

1. Confirm the contracting legal entity, ownership/control structure, principal
   office, operating history, revenue, tax filings, insurance, and current
   registrations.
2. Establish the authoritative NAICS, PSC, NIGP, service-region, and capability
   lists.
3. Reconcile the public capability statement, SAM/SBS profile, CMBL profile,
   buyer portals, and CRM so they describe the same company.
4. Create a secure evidence index for formation documents, tax records,
   ownership evidence, insurance, references, past performance, certifications,
   resumes, and financial-capacity documents.
5. Store sensitive evidence outside Git; place only status and secure-location
   references in the tracker.

**Exit condition:** one reviewed company profile and evidence index can support
every certification and buyer packet without recreating facts.

## Workstream 2: Prioritize certifications by revenue route

**Timing:** Weeks 1-3

Use this sequence:

| Priority | Lane | Decision gate | Revenue connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Houston METRO SBE | Confirm certificate proof, approved work codes, portal profile, and renewal date | METRO prime opportunities, certified-vendor searches, primes seeking SBE participation, and mentor-protege access |
| 1 | City of Houston OBO | Determine which of MBE/WBE/SBE/PDBE/DBE/ACDBE applies under current rules; complete the existing application lane | City solicitations, certified-firm directory, BeaconBid, departments, airports, and prime subcontracting |
| 1 | CMBL | Verify active status, NIGP codes, contacts, service regions, and annual renewal | State buyer visibility and bid invitations |
| 2 | Texas HUB/VetHUB | Complete a current eligibility review with UH APEX; do not assume qualification from the source chart | State agency/university visibility and state subcontracting plans |
| 2 | WOSB/EDWOSB | Verify ownership, control, citizenship, size, eligible NAICS, and economic criteria | Federal set-asides and prime-contractor goal credit |
| 2 | WBENC/WBEA | Compare cost and effort with a named corporate supplier-diversity target list | Corporate and participating government supplier-diversity channels |
| 3 | 8(a) | Review social/economic-disadvantage and potential-for-success requirements with UH APEX | Federal business development, set-aside, sole-source, and mentor-protege routes |
| 3 | HUBZone | Check the current principal-office and employee residency map before further work | Federal HUBZone set-asides and prime-contractor goal credit |
| Conditional | VOSB/SDVOSB | Pursue only if ownership/control and veteran-status requirements are met | Federal veteran contracting channels |

For each lane, record:

- eligibility decision: `likely`, `uncertain`, `not currently eligible`, or
  `certified`;
- official source and date checked;
- buyer and prime-contractor channels unlocked;
- required and missing documents;
- owner, due date, portal, application state, and next action;
- submission confirmation, approval evidence, and renewal date as separate
  milestones.

**Exit condition:** every certification lane has an explicit go/defer/no-go
decision and a buyer-linked business reason.

## Workstream 3: Build the buyer and prime account map

**Timing:** Weeks 2-5, then ongoing

### Buyer list

Start with:

- Houston METRO;
- City of Houston and Houston Airport System;
- University of Houston and other Texas universities;
- HISD and other target school systems;
- Port Houston;
- Harris County and adjacent counties;
- H-GAC and regional authorities;
- selected Texas agencies with matching historical spend;
- selected federal agencies with matching award history.

For each buyer, capture:

- procurement and certification portals;
- forecast, solicitation, and award-history sources;
- small-business office and program contacts;
- NAICS/NIGP categories purchased;
- incumbent and prime contractors;
- upcoming events, pre-bid meetings, and matchmakers;
- current opportunities and next action.

### Prime-contractor list

Use:

- SBA's directory of federal primes with subcontracting plans;
- SUBNet;
- USAspending and agency award history;
- GSA and DOT subcontracting directories;
- METRO, City, state, university, port, and county award records;
- certified-vendor and mentor-protege programs;
- UH APEX introductions and events.

Prioritize primes that:

1. already hold work with a target buyer;
2. need LeCrown's service categories or delivery geography;
3. maintain small-business subcontracting goals;
4. have an identifiable supplier-diversity contact and program manager;
5. have an upcoming recompete, option period, task order, or active
   subcontract need.

**Exit condition:** 20 buyers and 25 primes are ranked, assigned, and linked to
a specific revenue hypothesis.

## Workstream 4: Package the offer

**Timing:** Weeks 2-6

Create one reusable base packet and tailored variants:

1. one-page core capability statement;
2. Houston/METRO local-government version;
3. Texas state and university version;
4. federal-prime subcontracting version;
5. commercial supplier-diversity version;
6. targeted past-performance sheets;
7. reference list with permission and current contact information;
8. short prime-contractor outreach letter based on the APEX template;
9. secure evidence index rather than attaching sensitive documents by default.

Each variant must state:

- the exact problem and work package LeCrown can perform;
- relevant NAICS/NIGP and approved certification work codes;
- capacity, geography, insurance, and delivery constraints;
- specific past performance and measurable outcomes;
- the requested next step.

**Exit condition:** an operator can assemble a buyer- or prime-specific packet
in less than one business day.

## Workstream 5: Run a weekly pursuit cadence

**Timing:** Start Week 2

### Monday: discover and rank

- refresh Texas, federal, SUBNet, Gmail RFQ, and tracked local sources;
- add buyer events, matchmakers, forecasts, and prime outreach triggers;
- score opportunities and assign an initial route.

### Tuesday: qualify

- use the solicitation matrix;
- identify mandatory requirements, dealbreakers, capital needs, competition,
  delivery risk, and pricing questions;
- make a documented prime/sub/no-bid decision.

### Wednesday: package and partner

- tailor the capability statement and evidence packet;
- identify primes, subs, suppliers, and technical partners;
- request clarification or attend pre-bid/site-visit events.

### Thursday: outreach

- contact the buyer's small-business office when appropriate;
- contact both the prime's supplier-diversity team and relevant program team;
- ask for a specific meeting, work package, registration step, or introduction.

### Friday: follow up and learn

- update the CRM and certification tracker;
- schedule the next action;
- record blockers, losses, no-bids, and lessons;
- update buyer affinity and matching keywords.

**Exit condition:** every active pursuit has an owner, route, next action, and
date; no opportunity is active only because it appeared in a feed.

## Workstream 6: Use organizations as business-development channels

**Timing:** Ongoing

Maintain an engagement calendar for:

- UH APEX one-on-one advising and training;
- SBA Houston District and federal small-business events;
- METRO Help Center, assessments, training, and Interagency Mentor Protege
  Program;
- City of Houston OBO workshops and certified-firm outreach;
- Texas Economic Opportunity Forums and agency HUB events;
- WBEA/WBENC procurement and supplier-diversity events if eligible;
- agency industry days, pre-bid conferences, matchmakers, and prime outreach
  events.

Every event should have a business-development objective, target contacts, a
follow-up owner, and a seven-day follow-up deadline.

## Platform backlog

The existing platform already tracks opportunities, sources, certification
lanes, buyer gates, reusable documents, and capability-statement variants.
Extend it in this order:

1. add secure-location references for source documents and evidence without
   storing sensitive files or credentials;
2. connect certification lanes to the buyers and primes they unlock;
3. add prime-contractor account records and contacts;
4. attach a completed solicitation-review decision to each pursuit;
5. add outreach, event, and next-action history;
6. track outcomes and reasons for win, loss, no-bid, and defer;
7. report the success measures in this plan without conflating registrations,
   applications, certifications, submissions, and awards.

## First ten actions

1. Save and link the METRO certificate proof; confirm its approved work codes.
2. Continue the City of Houston OBO application and record the exact category
   and missing evidence.
3. Verify SAM.gov, UEI, and Small Business Search profile status.
4. Verify CMBL, NIGP codes, contacts, and renewal.
5. Hold a UH APEX eligibility review covering WOSB/EDWOSB, Texas HUB, 8(a),
   HUBZone, and WBENC/WBEA.
6. Select the first ten target buyers from platform sources and historical
   spend.
7. Select the first ten primes from SBA directories, SUBNet, and buyer award
   records.
8. Produce the federal-prime and Houston/METRO capability-statement variants.
9. Run four opportunities through the solicitation matrix and record the
   pursuit decision.
10. Start the weekly pursuit cadence and publish the first 30-day scorecard.

## Review points

- **Day 15:** company truth set, evidence index, certification eligibility
  screen, and first buyer/prime list reviewed.
- **Day 30:** registrations verified, priority certification next actions
  underway, first four opportunity reviews complete, and outreach cadence
  active.
- **Day 60:** buyer and prime maps expanded, tailored packets in use, and
  submissions or formal teaming discussions recorded.
- **Day 90:** review awards, pipeline quality, response rates, losses, and
  certification value; continue only the channels producing credible access.

This plan was prepared on July 22, 2026. Program eligibility and requirements
must be rechecked on the official sources before submission.
